Privacy policy

A plain-English explanation of what information this site collects, why, and how it's used. Last updated August 2026.

What we collect

This site is mostly static — reading it doesn't require an account and doesn't hand us any personal information. The only information we collect directly is what you choose to give us:

  • Newsletter signup. If you subscribe, we collect your email address so we can send the newsletter. Signups are processed by Beacons, a third-party platform — see their own privacy policy for how they handle that data.
  • Purchases. If you buy the book bundle, checkout is handled entirely by Beacons. We don't receive or store your payment details, card number, or billing address — that transaction happens on their platform, under their privacy policy.
  • Email you send us. If you email us directly, we keep that correspondence to respond to you and to improve the site.

Cookies and tracking

We don't run advertising trackers or tracking pixels on this site. Your web browser and hosting provider may log basic technical information (like IP address and browser type) automatically as part of normal web traffic — this is standard server behavior, not something we set up to track you individually.

Third-party links

This site links out to Amazon, TikTok Shop, and Beacons for purchases, and to other sites we reference. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we're not responsible for how they handle your information once you leave our site. Some of those links are affiliate links — see our affiliate disclosure for details.

How we use what we collect

We use your email address only to send the newsletter you signed up for, and to respond if you write to us. We don't sell, rent, or trade your information to anyone.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the unsubscribe link in any email we send. To ask us to delete correspondence you've sent us, email info@quickwellnesstips.com.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the "last updated" date above.

Questions

Email us at info@quickwellnesstips.com with any privacy questions.
