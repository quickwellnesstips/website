Purpose of this disclosure

This page explains, in full, how QuickWellnessTips ("QuickWellnessTips," "we," "us," "our") makes money from the product links on quickwellnesstips.com (the "Site"), consistent with the Federal Trade Commission's guidelines on endorsements and affiliate marketing. We believe you should always know when a recommendation comes with a financial relationship attached, so you can weigh it accordingly. This disclosure should be read together with our Privacy Policy, Medical Disclaimer, and Terms of Service.

FTC compliance

The FTC requires that any material connection between a publisher and the brands or retailers it links to be clearly and conspicuously disclosed. In line with that requirement: product links in our Featured section and elsewhere on the Site may be affiliate links, meaning we may earn a commission on qualifying purchases made through them, at no additional cost to you. This disclosure applies to every affiliate link on the Site, whether or not it is individually labeled.

Amazon Associates Program

QuickWellnessTips is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com and affiliated sites. As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases.

When you click an Amazon link on this Site and purchase a product within Amazon's applicable referral window, Amazon pays us a small commission. This applies not only to the specific product you clicked on, but, under Amazon's program terms, can extend to other items you add to your cart during that same shopping session. The price you pay is unchanged — you pay the same as if you had navigated to Amazon directly without going through our link.

TikTok Shop Affiliate Program

We are also a participant in the TikTok Shop Affiliate Program. The same core principle applies: when you tap through from one of our product cards or social posts and complete a purchase on TikTok Shop, we may earn a commission from that platform. As with Amazon, this comes at no additional cost to you — the price you pay is the price set by the seller on TikTok Shop, with no markup added by us.

Other and future affiliate programs

From time to time, we may join additional affiliate networks or brand-specific affiliate programs beyond Amazon and TikTok Shop. Regardless of which specific program a link belongs to, the same disclosure applies: any product link on this Site may be an affiliate link, and we may be compensated if you make a purchase through it. We are not obligated to individually re-list every affiliate network we join, but this disclosure is intended to cover all of them, present and future, under the same terms described here.

How affiliate links actually work

When you click an affiliate link, your browser is typically given a small tracking identifier (often stored as a temporary cookie) that tells the retailer's system the referral came from us. If you complete a qualifying purchase within that retailer's tracking window, the retailer pays us a percentage of the sale as a referral fee. We never see your payment information, your name, or your shipping address as part of this process — that data stays entirely with the retailer and is subject to their own privacy policy, not ours. See our Privacy Policy for more on what we do and don't collect.

What we promise

  • We only feature products we actually use ourselves and genuinely recommend.
  • We never accept payment, free product, or sponsorship in exchange for a placement in the Featured section.
  • We never promote a product we would not recommend to a friend.
  • Our opinions and recommendations are our own and are not dictated, reviewed, or approved by Amazon, TikTok Shop, or any brand before publication.
  • If a featured product changes quality, gets discontinued, or becomes something we no longer use, we remove it.

How product cards are chosen

Each entry in the Featured section is one we personally picked, then linked to an Amazon or TikTok Shop listing. The link is an affiliate link. If you would prefer to skip the affiliate step, you can always search for the product name directly on the retailer's site or app — you will not be charged more or less either way. We hope the curation is worth the trip; if not, you have not lost anything by using our link instead of searching yourself.

Sponsored content and gifted products

As of the date at the top of this page, QuickWellnessTips does not run paid or sponsored placements, and no brand has paid for or provided free product in exchange for inclusion on this Site. If that ever changes, any sponsored content, paid placement, or product provided to us free of charge in exchange for coverage will be clearly and separately labeled as such at the point where it appears, in addition to this general affiliate disclosure.

Book purchases

The digital e-book library is sold through our own storefront (the "Buy the Bundle" link). That is a direct sale between you and QuickWellnessTips, processed by our checkout provider, Beacons. There is no affiliate component to that transaction, and the library itself contains no affiliate links inside the PDFs.

We do not manufacture, test, or independently verify the products we link to, and we are not responsible for their quality, safety, or performance. See our Medical Disclaimer for more on the limits of any product-related content on this Site, particularly for supplements and wellness products.

Prices, availability, and product details shown on Amazon, TikTok Shop, or any other retailer are controlled entirely by that retailer and can change at any time without notice to us. We make no guarantee that the price or availability you see when you click through will match what was true when we originally featured the product, and we are not responsible for pricing errors, stock issues, or listing changes made by a third-party retailer.
