# Sour Cherry Tonic for the Late-Night Craving

A 5-ingredient switch for the 11pm sweet tooth. Tart, slightly bitter, ready in 4 minutes.

## What you need

- 8 oz tart cherry juice (the unsweetened kind, not the cocktail one)
- 1 oz apple cider vinegar
- A few shakes of ground ginger
- A pinch of sea salt
- Cold sparkling water, to top

## How to make it

Stir the cherry juice, vinegar, ginger, and salt in a tall glass. Add ice. Top with sparkling water. Drink it slow.

## Why it works

Tart cherry has natural melatonin-precursor compounds. The vinegar and ginger blunt the sugar spike. And the cold, fizzy water signals "this is the last thing you eat tonight" to the brain, which is half the problem solved.
