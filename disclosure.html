Affiliate disclosure

A plain-English explanation of how we earn money when you click the product links on this site, and a promise about which products make it onto the page.

Amazon Associates program

QuickWellnessTips is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by linking to Amazon.com and affiliated sites.

When you click an Amazon link on this site and purchase a product within a short window, Amazon pays us a small referral fee. The price you pay is unchanged. You pay the same as if you'd gone to Amazon directly.

Other affiliate programs

We are also participants in additional affiliate networks, including the TikTok Shop Affiliate Program. The same principle applies: when you tap through from one of our product cards and complete a purchase, we may earn a commission. Same price for you, no markup from us, no extra code to enter, no special link to follow.

What we promise

  • We only feature products we actually use ourselves or that fit the routines described in the books.
  • We never accept payment, free product, or sponsorship in exchange for a placement.
  • We never promote a product we would not recommend to a friend.
  • If a featured product changes quality, gets discontinued, or becomes something we no longer use, we remove it.

How product cards are chosen

Each entry in the Featured section is one we personally picked, then linked to an Amazon or TikTok Shop listing. The link is an affiliate link. If you would prefer to skip the affiliate step, you can always search for the product name directly on the retailer. We hope the curation is worth the trip; if not, you have not lost anything either way.

The digital library is sold through our own storefront (the "Buy the Bundle" link in the top right). That is a direct sale. There is no affiliate component. The library contains no affiliate links inside the PDFs.
